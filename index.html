    The Data Modernist

    Welcome to The Data Modernist — a space where technology, architecture, and human stories converge.
    I’m Hitesh, a Data & Solution Architect with over 18 years of experience designing data platforms, shaping information flows, and bridging the gap between business challenges and technical solutions.

    Why "The Data Modernist"?

    Modernism in art and design was about breaking away from tradition and creating something purposeful, clean, and timeless.
    I see the same shift happening in the world of data: moving from legacy silos and rigid processes toward open, adaptive, and intelligent platforms that empower people.

    This blog is my canvas to share:

    • Practical insights on distributed data platforms, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, and the modern lakehouse.
    • Architecture blueprints and industry reference models — with a focus on mining, GIS, and industrial IoT.
    • Personal experiments in AI and open-source projects (like The Data Load Tool).
    • Notes and reflections — sometimes just raw takeaways from my learning journey.

    Recent Work & Experience

    Some highlights from my recent projects:

    • Mining Client – Designed and delivered a Microsoft Fabric–based Contractor Analytics solution to automate data cleansing and streamline reporting for site superintendents.
    • Mining Client – Built a Fabric MVP and production deployment, including training to help their teams evolve the platform.
    • Another Mining Client – Established API ingestion patterns on Databricks for the G360 Risk platform, integrating with existing DevOps and Asset Bundles.
    • Retail Client – Supported Fabric implementation and data architecture as part of a multi-region rollout.

    Each project has been an opportunity to test modern data thinking against real-world complexity.

    Who is this blog for?

    • Data professionals curious about distributed architectures, cloud costs, and practical patterns.
    • Solution architects looking for reference designs and lessons from the field.
    • Career explorers navigating tech burnout, sabbaticals, or pivots into new roles.
    • Anyone modernist at heart — breaking tradition, experimenting, and reimagining what’s possible.

    What’s Next?

    I’ll be publishing essays, walkthroughs, and thought pieces regularly.
    If you’re into data, design, or the messy middle where technology meets human ambition — you’re in the right place.

    Stay tuned, subscribe, and let’s explore the modern data journey together.
