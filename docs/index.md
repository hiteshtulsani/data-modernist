# The Data Modernist

Welcome to **The Data Modernist** — a space where technology, architecture, and human stories converge.  
I’m Hitesh, a **Data & Solution Architect** with over 18 years of experience designing data platforms, shaping information flows, and bridging the gap between business challenges and technical solutions.  

---

## Why "The Data Modernist"?

Modernism in art and design was about **breaking away from tradition** and creating something purposeful, clean, and timeless.  
I see the same shift happening in the world of data: moving from legacy silos and rigid processes toward **open, adaptive, and intelligent platforms** that empower people.  

This blog is my canvas to share:

- **Practical insights** on distributed data platforms, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, and the modern lakehouse.  
- **Architecture blueprints** and industry reference models — with a focus on mining, GIS, and industrial IoT.  
- **Personal experiments** in AI and open-source projects (like [The Data Load Tool](#)).  
- **Notes and reflections** — sometimes just raw takeaways from my learning journey.

## Recent Work & Experience

Each project has been an opportunity to test **modern data thinking against real-world complexity**.
